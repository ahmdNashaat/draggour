# Project Brief

## 1. Client
Dr. Mohamed Aggour, consultant interventional neuroradiologist.

## 2. Website objective
The website should:
- represent the doctor's professional level
- make his identity and activity easy to discover through Google, AI platforms and social media
- give patients and referring physicians a clear next step
- provide a simple remote-consultation request flow
- present biography and professional activities clearly
- direct physician education primarily to YouTube
- remain simple to operate

## 3. Client preferences
- Elegant
- Professional
- Simple
- Clean
- Calm
- No unnecessary complexity
- No animation-heavy website
- No heavy 3D as a requirement
- No complex medical portal
- Human review before appointment/payment
- WhatsApp is a primary communication channel

## 4. Audiences
Primary:
- Patients / families
- Physicians / referring professionals

Secondary:
- Hospitals / healthcare organisations
- Conference / media / academic contacts

## 5. V1 website sections
### Home
Hero with doctor's name, logo, portrait, professional title and short statement.
Concise entry points to Biography, Conditions, E-learning, Professional Activities & Media, and Remote Consultation.

### Biography
Full professional profile containing approved:
- Career
- Experience
- Current positions
- Qualifications
- Professional societies
- Teaching
- Academic work
- Selected research/publications
- Professional milestones

### Conditions
Six initial condition destinations:
- Brain Aneurysm
- Stroke
- AVM
- Carotid Stenosis
- Venous Sinus Disorders
- Chronic Subdural Haematoma

Keep them concise and oriented toward the appropriate consultation path rather than large educational articles.

### E-learning for Physicians
Link/curate YouTube educational content. Do not build a video-hosting platform.

### Professional Activities & Media
Selected conferences, professional societies, invited talks and media appearances.

### Remote Consultation
The highest-priority functional journey.

### Contact
Clear enquiry paths without creating a complex CRM.

### Legal / Privacy
Privacy, terms/disclaimer/cookie wording as approved.

## 6. Current consultation decisions
- Exactly two top-level requester types:
  1. Patient / Consultation
  2. Physician / Referral
- Then: Emergency / Non-Emergency
- If Non-Emergency:
  - Online Consultation
  - Clinic Visit
- Medical attachments:
  - PDF
  - JPG
  - PNG
  - maximum 3 files
- Payment happens after staff review and appointment confirmation.
- Secretary may handle contact, booking and payment coordination.
- WhatsApp automation is required.
- Initial WhatsApp automation confirms receipt and says the team will respond as soon as possible.

## 7. V1 data decision
Current decision:
- no persistent request history
- no database
- no patient accounts
- no admin dashboard

This does not mean sensitive files may be casually emailed. If medical uploads are implemented directly, use an approved secure mechanism. See `SECURITY_AND_PRIVACY.md`.

## 8. Future-ready principle
Possible later additions:
- Admin dashboard
- request history
- managed content system
- database
- status management
- internal booking/payment workflows

Do not build these now unless requirements change.
